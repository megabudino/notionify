## Codebase Patterns
- Pandoc task lists use `.task-list` and `.task-list-item` classes for checkbox styling.
- Dependency checks in `notionify.sh` use `ensure_dependency` with OS-specific installers.
- Bundled assets like `notion.css` are resolved relative to `notionify.sh` via `BASH_SOURCE`.
